    How the signals work

    Everything starts in “wait” on purpose. A signal comes only from where the price sits against levels calculated from each stock’s baseline (the snapshot price, or whatever you last set). The template flags entries after a pullback, not after a jump. News and catalysts are context to check before acting; they never change a signal by themselves.

    These percentages are a discipline template, not forecasts: they make you decide your entry, exit and loss limit before the price moves. Stop, trim and take-profit signals only matter if you hold the stock. Analyst ratings come from the quote pages and are not recommendations.
